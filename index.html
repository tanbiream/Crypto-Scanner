Candles are built from one sampled last-trade price per minute, so they have no wicks. Volume, open interest and mark price are only updated by the exchange at daily settlement, so volume-based signals are unavailable. Indicators show "warming up" until enough history exists. "Quiet" means no recent trade, so no reading is given. These are technical readings, not predictions. Leveraged futures can lose more than you put in. This is not financial advice.
