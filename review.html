Radar Review

Replays recorded Kraken spot snapshots with the shared rules (), finds every flag, Watch entry and pullback setup using only data available at that moment, then measures what price did afterward. Analysis only.
Pass/fail bars, written before more data arrives (cost 1.0% round trip, 3-hour hold for Watch):
Watch (each direction): (1) at least 50 completed entries spread over at least 20 different hours; (2) hit rate at least 1.5x the random-coin hit rate; (3) average move in the watched direction minus cost above zero, and above the random-coin average in the same direction; (4) average after cost above zero in BOTH halves of the period.
Pullback: (1) at least 50 completed setups spread over at least 7 different days; (2) waiting for the pullback zone averages above zero per setup after cost and beats chasing; (3) above zero in BOTH halves.
These numbers were set after seeing one day of Watch results and the first pullback results, so they are not perfectly blind. Meeting a bar is not proof of a profitable strategy.

How to read this: entry is the recorded price one minute after the signal. The cost is an assumption you can change, not a measured fee, and thin coins can cost more. "Baseline" is how random liquid coins at sampled times did in the same period, so a falling or rising market is not credited to a signal. For the pullback test, fills are assumed at exactly the zone price and stops at exactly the invalidation price, which is optimistic; setups that never fill count as zero. Short ideas are hypothetical because spot is not assumed to be shortable. A coin raises at most one signal of each kind per hour. Entries from different coins at the same time are correlated, which is why the bars count distinct hours and days. If "Rules start" is empty, the results include data used to design earlier rules and are not fair evidence. A few days, or one-directional markets, say little about other markets. Nothing here is financial advice.
