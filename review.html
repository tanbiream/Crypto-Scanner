How to read this: entry is the recorded price one minute after the flag; exit is the recorded price after the chosen holding time. "Direction" means: breakout, potential pump and extended up are treated as long ideas, breakdown, potential dump and extended down as short ideas (shorting spot is not assumed to be available, so short results are hypothetical). "Baseline" is the average move of all liquid coins at sampled times in the same period, in the same direction, so you can see whether a flag did better than random coins in the same market. The cost is an assumption you can change, not a measured fee, and thin coins can cost more. A coin can raise at most one flag per hour. Samples from a few days, or a weekend, say little about other markets. A positive result here is not proof of a profitable strategy, and nothing here is financial advice.
