Radar Review

Replays recorded Kraken spot snapshots with the shared rules (), finds every flag, Watch entry and pullback setup using only data available at that moment, then measures what price did afterward. Analysis only.
Press "Run review". Set "Rules start" to the commit time of rules.js.

How to read this: entry is the recorded price one minute after the signal. The cost is an assumption you can change, not a measured fee, and thin coins can cost more. "Baseline" is how often random liquid coins at sampled times did the same thing in the same period. For the pullback test, fills are assumed at exactly the zone price and stops at exactly the invalidation price, which is optimistic. Short ideas are hypothetical because spot is not assumed to be shortable. A coin raises at most one signal of each kind per hour. If "Rules start" is empty, the results include data that was used to design earlier rules and are not fair evidence. A few days, or a weekend, say little about other markets. A positive result here is not proof of a profitable strategy, and nothing here is financial advice.
