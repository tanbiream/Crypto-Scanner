Score Review (replay of LONG / SHORT leans)

Replays recorded Bitnomial perpetual snapshots with the frozen score rules (). At each 5-minute step it scores every coin using only data from the previous 24 hours, exactly as the live scoreboard does, finds LONG and SHORT leans, and measures what price did afterward. Analysis only.
Pass/fail rules, written before any results: the primary test is the 1-hour holding time. A lean type "meets the bar" only if ALL of these hold: (1) at least 50 completed events spread over at least 20 different hours; (2) the average result after cost is above zero; (3) it beats the baseline of random scored coins in the same direction; (4) the average after cost is above zero in BOTH the first and second half of the test period. The cost to use for the official read is 0.5% round trip. Other holding times are exploratory only. Meeting the bar would still not be proof of a profitable strategy.

How to read this: entry is the recorded last-traded price one minute after the lean; exit is the recorded price after the holding time. The cost is an assumption, not a measured fee, and thin contracts can cost more. Short ideas are hypothetical. A coin can raise at most one lean per hour. "Market-wide" means at least 70% of scored coins leaned the same way at that moment (at least 5 scored): those leans mostly reflect the overall market, so the "not market-wide" rows show whether the scores add anything beyond market direction. Candles are built from one price sample per minute, so prices of thinly traded contracts can be stale, which makes results rougher than they look. Events from different coins at the same time are correlated, which is why the test counts distinct hours. If the test period is mostly one-directional, results mostly say how that kind of market behaved. Nothing here is financial advice.
