Flags show unusual trading activity that has already started. Watch means early signs (volume building while price is still quiet): it is a "keep an eye on this" list, not a prediction, and most Watch coins will go nowhere. Pullback levels are reference points based on one price sample per minute, not predictions: pumped coins often fall straight through them or never come back, and the invalidation level marks where the idea is wrong. All thresholds are untested first guesses. Low-volume coins can be manipulated. Kraken's daily counters reset at 00:00 UTC (8 PM ET before the clock change, 7 PM ET after). Leveraged trading can lose more than you put in. This is not financial advice.
