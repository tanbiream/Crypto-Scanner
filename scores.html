Scores measure how well the technicals line up (trend size, timeframe agreement, momentum, structure), minus a penalty when a coin has already moved far over 1 hour, 3 hours or the loaded history. They are NOT win probabilities and are untested. Not used: live volume, open interest, VWAP and ADX (unavailable at this data quality). Candles come from one price sample per minute, so they have no wicks. Coins that trade rarely are not scored. Reference levels appear only when the first target is at least 2x an assumed round-trip cost (an assumption, not a measured fee); the stop is the farther of the 1-hour swing point and 2.5x the recent average 5-minute move, and targets are 1x, 2x and 3x that distance: they are reference points, not predictions. "Trades seen" counts distinct last-trade times in the sampled data and understates real trade counts. When most coins lean the same way, that is usually one market-wide move. Leveraged futures can lose more than you put in. This is not financial advice.
