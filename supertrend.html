Supertrend Test (Kraken spot, daily, long-only)

Version . Analysis only, no trading.
Pre-registered rules and bar, written before any results (Nov 24, 2026):
Indicator: textbook Supertrend, ATR period 10 (Wilder smoothing), multiplier 3, on daily candles. Nothing is tuned.
Universe: Kraken USD spot pairs (stablecoins and fiat excluded) with a 20-day average daily volume of at least $300K on the signal day. Signals are ignored during each coin's first 30 candles.
Trades: long-only. Buy at the next day's open after the indicator flips from bearish to bullish. Sell at the next day's open after it flips back to bearish. Round-trip cost 1.0% (primary).
Variants: S1 plain. S2 enters only when Bitcoin's daily close is above its own 100-day simple average on the signal day (exits unchanged).
No train/holdout split (nothing is fitted). The 2026 data was seen in earlier tests, so a pass is provisional and must be confirmed on at least 8 weeks of new data.
A variant meets the bar only if ALL hold: (1) at least 200 completed trades over at least 12 distinct months; (2) average after cost of at least +0.5% and at least 1.0 percentage point above the random baseline (all liquid coin-days with the same holding time, on filter days for S2); (3) average after cost still above zero after removing the best 5% of trades; (4) average after cost above zero in BOTH halves; (5) at least 60% of months with 10 or more trades have a positive average. Meeting the bar is not proof. Any change becomes a new version (s2) and is logged.

Known biases: only coins listed on Kraken today are included (delisted or collapsed coins are missing, which flatters every rule). Fills are assumed exactly at the open price, which is optimistic; the 1.0% cost is meant to cover fees and slippage but thin coins can cost more. Trades still open at the end of the data are excluded (long winners are therefore under-counted). Trades from different coins on the same day are correlated, so the monthly checks matter more than the trade count. Daily candles start at 00:00 UTC. A baseline is the average gross return of every liquid coin-day with the same holding time (on Bitcoin-above-average days for S2). Nothing here is financial advice.
