Daily Swing Test (Kraken spot, long-only)

Version . Replays three fixed rules on about two years of Kraken daily candles. Analysis only, no trading.
Pre-registered rules and bar, written before any results (Nov 24, 2026; version d2 adds the market filter):
Universe: Kraken USD spot pairs (stablecoins and fiat excluded) with a 20-day average daily volume of at least $300K on the signal day. Long-only. Entry at the next day's open, exit at an open. Round-trip cost 1.0% (primary).
Market filter (new in d2): all three rules enter only on signal days when Bitcoin's daily close is above its own 100-day simple average (including the signal day). Exits are unchanged. The random baseline uses only those same days.
Rule 1, breakout: buy after a close above the prior 20-day high. Exit after a close below the prior 10-day low, or after 60 days.
Rule 2, weekly momentum: every Sunday close, buy the 5 coins with the best 28-day return (only if positive). Hold 7 days.
Rule 3, oversold bounce: buy after a drop of 20% or more over 5 days. Hold 5 days.
Split: before 2026-01-01 is training (for checking the code, run first). From 2026-01-01 is the holdout, run once per version after the rules are final.
A rule meets the bar on the holdout only if ALL hold: (1) at least 100 completed trades over at least 8 distinct months; (2) average after cost above zero and at least 0.5 percentage points above the random baseline; (3) average after cost still above zero after removing the best 5% of trades; (4) average after cost above zero in BOTH halves; (5) at least 60% of months with 5 or more trades have a positive average. Meeting the bar is not proof of a profitable strategy. Any change to a rule after seeing training results becomes a new version (d3) and is logged.
Press "Load data". If you loaded data in the last 12 hours it loads instantly from this browser; otherwise the first load takes about 5 minutes.

Known biases: only coins listed on Kraken today are included, so coins that were delisted or collapsed are missing (this flatters every rule, especially the oversold bounce). Fills are assumed exactly at the open price, which is optimistic; the 1.0% cost is meant to cover fees and slippage but thin coins can cost more. Trades unfinished at the end of the data are excluded. Trades from different coins on the same day are correlated, so the monthly checks matter more than the trade count. Daily candles start at 00:00 UTC. A baseline is the average gross return of every liquid coin-day (on Bitcoin-above-average days) with the same holding time in the same period. The market filter may leave too few trades or months, which is a valid result. Nothing here is financial advice.
